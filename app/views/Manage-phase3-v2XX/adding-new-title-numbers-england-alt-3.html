Where do I find title or folio numbers?

You can find a title number by downloading the title register or title plan from the UK land registry where the property or land is registered:

HM Land Registry (England and Wales)

Registers of Scotland

Land Registry of Northern Ireland

You'll need to pay a fee for each title register or title plan you download. The fee varies depending on the land registry.
